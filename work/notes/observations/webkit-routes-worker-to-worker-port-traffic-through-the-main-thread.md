# WebKit routes worker-to-worker MessagePort traffic through the main thread

2026-09-27. Measured by `packages/webevm/test/serve-on-port.spec.ts` (task `a-worker-node-serves-eip-1193-on-a-handed-port`): with a `MessageChannel` made on the page and its two ends transferred into two workers, a consumer worker got about 100 000 answers from the node worker during a one-second busy loop on the page on Chromium, and ZERO on WebKit (all of them arrived the moment the loop ended). No page code relays anything on either engine (the page's handles on both workers saw zero messages meanwhile); WebKit's engine carries the port messages through the main thread itself. Worth knowing for `wighawag/etherfold`'s browser indexer (`a-worker-host-takes-its-provider-and-settings-from-the-tab`): on Safari a busy tab stalls the indexer's provider even though the indexer and the node are both workers. Recorded in the README's worker-to-worker section too.
