**Impersonation, opt-in: `hardhat_impersonateAccount` / `hardhat_stopImpersonatingAccount` and their `anvil_` names.** With the new `impersonation: true` option, the four methods maintain a set of impersonated addresses (each returns `null`, as anvil answers them), and `eth_sendTransaction` from one is filled as a dev account's is, given a fabricated signature unique per sender (a fixed low `s`, an `r` derived from the address, so two senders' otherwise identical transactions get two hashes), and sent through webevm's `evm_sendRawTransactionAs`, executing as that address on either engine. The option needs a `senderMode: 'trusted'` node: `createDevNode({impersonation: true})` creates one and refuses an explicit `senderMode: 'recover'`; `wrapDevNode(node, {impersonation: true})` throws at once on any other node. While off (the default), the four methods answer `-32601` naming the option. Every signing method refuses an impersonated address with `4100` (no key is held for it); a dev account that is also impersonated still signs and sends with its key. The `4100` refusal of an unknown `from` now names `hardhat_impersonateAccount` (or `anvil_impersonateAccount`) for that address, or the option while it is off. A dump holding impersonated transactions is not replayable on a `'recover'` node.
